### About Me

I am a 21-year-old Software Engineer based in Madeira, Portugal. I hold a **B.Sc. in Computer Science and Informatics Engineering** and am currently pursuing my **M.Sc. in Computer Science and Informatics Engineering** at the **University of Madeira (UMa)**.
